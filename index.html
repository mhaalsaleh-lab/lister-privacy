Privacy policy: Lister

Lister is a personal tool used by its owner to manage their own eBay listings.

What it accesses

The seller's own eBay listings, including titles, prices, quantities, conditions, categories, descriptions and item numbers. It does not access buyer information, payment details or any other eBay account data.

Where data is stored

On the owner's computer only. Access tokens are kept in a local configuration file and are not shared with anyone.

What it does not do

It does not sell, share or send data to third parties, and it does not use data for advertising.
